## Console Streaming Overview

This independent guide starts with the scene you want viewers to see. The HDMI card supplies the console picture and audio, while Streamlabs Desktop arranges the picture, camera and overlays. A second scene can hide gameplay while keeping conversation or break graphics on screen.

Use local recording to check framing, mic levels and lip sync without sending video to a channel. When the recording works, connect the streaming account and test your alerts. Treat the card feed and the alert feed as separate inputs so you can troubleshoot one without tearing down the other.

An optional higher tier adds expanded multistreaming and extra overlay tools. Compare reports on similar hardware, especially if you plan to run animated widgets while encoding. A general-purpose mixer is an alternative for creators who would rather assemble their own layout and integrations.

## Getting Started Playbook

Connect the console to the capture card's HDMI In and hook up the card to your computer. In your gameplay scene, add one Video Capture Device for the card and another for the webcam. Add your microphone, position the camera and make sure the game audio is not being captured twice.

Set a recording destination under Output and record a short rehearsal. Play back the file to check the camera, voice, console sound and scene changes. If you're going live, connect your channel, add Alert Box and use Test Widgets to check placement and volume before you start.

## Everyday Use

Open the app on your gameplay scene, check the meters, and confirm which microphone is selected. Local recording does not need a Streamlabs account login. Sign in and connect the relevant platform for account-dependent features like alerts and channel integrations. Before going live, check the stream destination and title as carefully as the camera framing.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

Install issues? Confirm OS support and grant camera and microphone permissions when needed.  
Performance problems? Test with animated overlays off and lower output resolution or frame rate if the encoder stalls.  
Edition differences? The core app runs without a higher tier; sign in only for account-dependent features such as alerts.  
Compatibility? Close any vendor app using the HDMI card and check that the card exposes a supported video source.  
Update failures? Note the build, save local recordings, and retry using the publisher's installer for your OS.
